i'm interested in how good products are built, from understanding the problem to engineering a solution that people can actually use.

i like starting with real problems, figuring out why they are difficult, and exploring what a useful solution should look like before deciding how to build it.

when developing software, i'm drawn to the details that make a product dependable: how it handles failures, manages data, coordinates background work, and behaves under unexpected conditions.

i learn by building complete projects, making design decisions, testing assumptions, and understanding the trade-offs behind the systems i create.

my goal is to become a product-minded software engineer who can connect user needs with strong engineering fundamentals and turn ideas into dependable software.

my projects are where i put this approach into practice.
